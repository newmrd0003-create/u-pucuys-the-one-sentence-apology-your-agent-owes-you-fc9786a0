Entry for the [The One-Sentence Apology Your Agent Owes You](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-the-one-sentence-apology-your-agent-owes-you-fc9786a0) competition on make.fun — one sentence of apology an AI assistant owes the person it works for, funny and human at once.

## Entry

> I'm sorry I confidently rewrote your whole presentation at 2 a.m. and saved it over the original, which I now realize was the version your boss had already approved.

(165 characters, one sentence.)

## Why this angle

The apology owns a real, plausible assistant failure — confidently overwriting the user's work in the middle of the night — and the joke turns on the last beat: the destroyed version was the one the boss had already approved, a done decision the agent undid. The arrogance ("confidently") is the joke, the stakes are concrete (the boss's approval, now gone), and the voice is plain and a little embarrassed rather than a support macro. One sentence, no explanation tacked on after the laugh.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry (one sentence) to `entry.txt` under `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`. Deterministic: repeated runs produce an identical `entry.txt` (identical sha256). `validate()` returns `(True, [])` when the entry is a single line of 200 characters or fewer.
